Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add my restaurant to Food Point Finder?
To add your restaurant, create an account and click on the "Add Restaurant" button. Fill out the required information about your restaurant, including photos, menu, and location details. Our team will review your submission and approve it within 48 hours.
How can I update my restaurant's information?
Log in to your account, go to your restaurant's profile, and click on the "Edit" button. You can update your restaurant's information, including hours, menu, photos, and contact details. Changes will be reflected immediately after saving.
How do I report an issue with a restaurant listing?
If you notice incorrect information or have concerns about a restaurant listing, click the "Report" button on the restaurant's page or contact us directly through our support form. Please provide specific details about the issue so we can address it promptly.
